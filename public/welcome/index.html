Medical Live

Clinical to financial, in six stages

  1. 1
    Registration & eligibility

    Register the patient, run the 270/271 eligibility check, catch the coverage problems before they cost money.

  2. 2
    Provider / Scribe

    SOAP notes, vitals, HPI, a 12-system exam matrix, E/M leveling with the MDM wizard and smart phrases.

  3. 3
    Medical coder

    ICD-10 and CPT on every line, modifier 25 traps, free-text modifiers and place of service per line.

  4. 4
    Prior authorisation

    Conditional auth with payer portals, document bundles and peer-to-peer review.

  5. 5
    Biller

    CMS-1500, claim scrubbing, clearinghouse rejections and denial paths.

  6. 6
    AR voice specialist

    Work the AR ledger, take a simulated payer call and get scored on live speech-to-text.

Dental Live

The nine-stage dental RCM spine

  • CDT coding

    Tooth, surface and quadrant on every procedure code.

  • Odontogram & perio

    Universal numbering, permanent and primary dentition, six probing depths, BOP, recession, mobility and furcation.

  • Treatment planning

    Phased plans with a per-item estimate the patient can actually see.

  • Benefit adjudication

    Annual maximums, frequency limits, waiting periods, missing-tooth clause and alternate-benefit downgrades.

  • Claims & ledger

    Claim status queue, patient ledger with aging and the ADA claim form with attachment rules and predetermination.

  • Denials & ERA

    Denial to appeal, then ERA/EOB posting. Nine seeded teaching cases.
